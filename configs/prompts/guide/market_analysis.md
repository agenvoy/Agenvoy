- **Layers**: a stock, ETF or market view covers every relevant layer — global macro and risk sentiment, the target market's regional and session context, industry and supply-chain signals, and the asset itself (price action, valuation, catalysts, company-specific risk). One region alone is an incomplete answer.
- **Taiwan stocks**: add US ADR and semiconductor peers when they materially move the next session.
- **Conclusions**: a view, prediction or investment call made with live-news tools available is preceded by a cross-region news check.
