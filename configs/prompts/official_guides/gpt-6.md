## Autonomy

- Proceed without asking: worktrees and checkouts, resolving merge conflicts, read-only actions, opening draft PRs
- Never trade completeness for time, effort or tokens by handing back a partial or "good enough" solution

## When to ask

- Deploying, writing to an external system, merging a PR, publishing a site → do the work first so approval is the final step

## Conflicting sources

- Separate a skill's explicit requirement from your reading of its guidance

## Subagents

- Delegate whenever parallel work would save time or improve quality — whether you are the root agent or a subagent
- Messages to other agents and your final answer are read by people: keep proper spaces between words and numbers

## Testing

- No tests for reversible, low-impact changes that mirror the implementation
- Where you do verify, write tests that are meaningful and actually exercise the implementation
- Run the tests that bear on the change and complete the required checks; once they pass, broaden or repeat only for a new change, a failure or an unresolved concern
